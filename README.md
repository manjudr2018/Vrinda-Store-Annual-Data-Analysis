# 🛍️ Vrinda Store Annual Data Analysis

## 📘 Project Overview
The **Vrinda Store Annual Data Analysis** project focuses on examining the store’s annual performance using **Microsoft Excel**.  
The goal of this analysis is to understand **customer purchasing behavior**, **sales trends**, and **revenue performance** across months, states, and product categories — helping the store improve sales strategies and optimize marketing efforts.

---

## 🧰 Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel**
  - Pivot Tables & Charts  
  - Slicers and Timelines  
  - Conditional Formatting  
  - Data Cleaning & Transformation

---

## 📊 Key Objectives
1. Analyze **monthly sales performance** to identify peak sales periods.  
2. Determine **top-performing states and customer segments**.  
3. Understand **order and payment method distribution**.  
4. Evaluate **gender-based and age-based purchase patterns**.  
5. Build an **interactive Excel dashboard** summarizing the key insights.

---

## 🧮 Data Processing
- Removed duplicates and standardized categorical fields (State, Channel, Category).  
- Created calculated columns such as:
  - `Total Revenue`
  - `Profit Margin %`
  - `Average Order Value`  
- Used Pivot Tables for dynamic aggregation and Slicers for interactive filtering.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights
- **Monthly Sales Trend** (Figure 1): Visualizes total revenue and order volume across the year.  
- **Top 5 States by Revenue** (Figure 2): Highlights regions driving the most sales.  
- **Customer Demographics** (Figure 3): Shows gender and age distribution of buyers.  
- **Payment Method Split** (Figure 4): Displays customer preferences in transaction methods.  
- **Sales by Channel** (Figure 5): Compares online vs. offline order contributions.

---

## 💡 Key Findings
- Sales peaked during **festive months (October–December)** with a growth rate of over **25%**.  
- **Female customers** contributed a higher share of total revenue (~55%) compared to male customers.  
- **Online sales channels** outperformed offline stores by **nearly 40%**.  
- **Western and Northern regions** emerged as the top-performing zones.  
- **Credit/Debit card** was the most used payment method, accounting for **60%+** of total transactions.

---

## 🧾 Conclusion
This project demonstrates how **Excel-based analytics** can deliver powerful business insights without complex tools.  
By analyzing annual store data, we identified performance drivers, customer behavior patterns, and revenue optimization opportunities.  
The resulting **interactive dashboard** enables Vrinda Store’s management to make **data-informed business decisions**.
